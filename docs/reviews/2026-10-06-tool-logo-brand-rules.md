# Tool logos on setup cards — official brand rules

Date: 2026-10-06. Research only. No code. No commit.

Question: can Agrypnos, an open-source Mac menu-bar extra, show third-party **graphic logos** next to setup cards for OpenCode, Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor (and, if official pages were easy to find, Telegram and Discord) without a trademark problem?

Agrypnos already uses the **plain names** of those tools to say it forwards questions or sends alerts for them. The user wants the artwork because the cards look empty.

This note records what each company’s **official brand, trademark, or press page** says. It is not legal advice. It does not invent a fair-use blessing. If a page does not publish a logo license, it says so.

Fetched 2026-10-06 from the live URLs below.

## Bottom line (setup-card logos)

| Mark | For Agrypnos setup-card logos | Official page |
|---|---|---|
| **OpenCode** | **No published license.** Brand kit exists; no third-party logo grant. | https://opencode.ai/brand |
| **Claude / Claude Code (Anthropic)** | **Not allowed** without written permission and prior approval of the materials. | https://www.anthropic.com/legal/trademark-guidelines |
| **Codex / OpenAI** | **Not allowed** without permission. Published guidelines exist; they tell third parties not to use the logo without permission and to email for logo permission. | https://openai.com/brand/ |
| **Cursor (Anysphere)** | **No published license** for a third-party app’s UI. Asset kit exists; no grant except Cursor Marketplace publishers. | https://cursor.com/brand |
| **Telegram** (if used on Notif cards) | **Conditional.** Press page allows logos for illustrations and “forward to Telegram” buttons if you are not representing Telegram. Do not use the official logo **as Agrypnos’s own mark**. | https://telegram.org/tour/screenshots |
| **Discord** (if used on Notif cards) | **Conditional.** Brand page lets you use the official logo (color / black / white), unmodified, on digital assets, to identify Discord — not to look like Discord built or sponsors Agrypnos. | https://discord.com/branding |

Putting the **picture** of a company’s logo on Agrypnos’s own cards is a different legal object from typing that company’s **name** in a sentence. See the Claude section below.

## Word versus logo (plain language)

Saying the words **“Claude Code”** on a setup card is naming the tool Agrypnos talks about. Anthropic’s Claude Code legal page **allows accurate plain-text** statements that a product has Claude Code preinstalled or that it **runs Claude Code**. That is identifying the product by its name.

Using **Anthropic’s logo artwork** (the Claude / Anthropic graphic) is using their **trademark drawing**, not just the name. Anthropic’s Trademark Guidelines say you may use their trademarks **only as they specifically permit**, and **only in materials they approve beforehand**. Putting their picture next to an Agrypnos card is not the plain-text name use they listed. It needs their written permission.

The same split is useful for the others even when their pages are thinner: **the name in a sentence is identification; the logo file is their artwork.** A downloadable brand zip is not, by itself, a license to ship that artwork inside Agrypnos.

## OpenCode (Anomaly Innovations)

**Final URL:** https://opencode.ai/brand  
**Also read:** https://opencode.ai/legal/terms-of-service  
**Software license (not a trademark license):** https://github.com/anomalyco/opencode/blob/dev/LICENSE (MIT)

### 1. Is the logo allowed for a third-party integrator?

**No published license.** The brand page offers PNG/SVG downloads and says they are “resources and assets to help you work with the OpenCode brand.” It does not say a third-party app may display those logos in its UI. It does not say “compatible with.” It does not grant permission.

### 2. What the official pages require

On https://opencode.ai/brand, the published text is only the heading, the one-line subtitle, and download buttons. There are no “do not modify,” “do not imply endorsement,” or written-permission rules on that page.

The Terms of Use (https://opencode.ai/legal/terms-of-service) say Anomaly Innovations, Inc. retains all right, title, and interest in the Services, and: “Except as expressly set forth herein, no rights to the Services or Third Party Models are granted to you.” They also forbid using the Services in a way that infringes OpenCode’s intellectual property. That is a reservation of rights, not a logo license.

The GitHub MIT license covers the **software** (copyright). It does not mention trademarks or the logo. MIT is not a trademark license.

### 3. If no official logo license is published

**None is published.** Do not treat the brand download as permission to ship the mark in Agrypnos.

## Claude Code / Anthropic

**Final URLs:**

- Trademark Guidelines: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/trademark-guidelines (effective August 1, 2024)
- Claude Code legal (name + logo): https://code.claude.com/docs/en/legal-and-compliance (same text at https://docs.anthropic.com/en/docs/claude-code/legal-and-compliance)
- Consumer Terms, “Use of our brand”: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/consumer-terms

### 1. Is the logo allowed for a third-party integrator?

**Not allowed** for Agrypnos setup-card artwork unless Anthropic gives **written permission** and **approves the materials first**. Accurate **plain text** that Agrypnos runs / talks to Claude Code is what they listed as OK. The logo is not.

### 2. What the official pages require

Claude Code legal page:

> You can accurately say, in plain text, that your product has Claude Code preinstalled or that it runs Claude Code. But you can’t use the Claude Code or Anthropic names or logos as part of your own product, feature, or company name, in your own logo, or in a way that suggests Anthropic built, endorses, or is partnered with your product. Any other use of Anthropic’s names or logos is governed by our Trademark Guidelines and requires our written permission.

Trademark Guidelines (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/trademark-guidelines):

- Use trademarks **only as specifically permitted** and **only in materials they approve beforehand**. They may terminate permission at any time.
- They supply the image and size/spacing rules. **No alterations** (color, font, proportion, or otherwise).
- Do **not** imply Anthropic sponsorship, endorsement, relationship, or affiliation except as they expressly authorize.
- Do not use the marks in a way that could damage Anthropic’s reputation or goodwill.
- Readable background; reasonable space around the mark; do not add a trademark symbol.
- Goodwill from use belongs to Anthropic.
- Existing business relationship + want to use marks in a press release, on a website, **or in another context**: email **marketing@anthropic.com**.

Consumer Terms (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/consumer-terms), “Use of our brand”:

> You may not, without our prior written permission, use our name, logos, or other trademarks in connection with products or services other than the Services, or in any other way that implies our affiliation, endorsement, or sponsorship. To seek permission, please email us at marketing@anthropic.com.

Agrypnos is not Anthropic’s Services. A Claude logo on an Agrypnos card is use **in connection with Agrypnos**.

https://claude.com/partners/powered-by-claude is a **curated list of businesses**. It is not a self-serve logo license for Agrypnos.

### 3. If no official logo license is published

A license **is** published. It is **permission-required**, prior-approval, no-endorsement, do-not-modify. It does **not** bless shipping the Claude artwork in a third-party menu-bar app.

## Codex / OpenAI

**Final URL:** https://openai.com/brand/  
No separate official Codex-only brand page was found. Codex is covered as an OpenAI product / other OpenAI trademark on that page.

### 1. Is the logo allowed for a third-party integrator?

**Not allowed without permission.** OpenAI publishes Design Guidelines for “partners, resellers, customers, developers, consultants, publishers, and any other third parties.” Those guidelines still say **do not use the logo without permission**, send **logo permission requests** to partnercomms@openai.com, and require an **approval process for co-branded materials**. They do not list “put our logo on your third-party app’s setup cards” as a permitted case.

### 2. What the official page requires

https://openai.com/brand/:

- OpenAI name, OpenAI logo, ChatGPT and GPT brands, **and other OpenAI trademarks** are OpenAI’s property.
- **Do:** use the logo only when it **directly relates to OpenAI services**; follow the style guides and usage terms; use the logo **exactly as provided** and acknowledge it belongs to OpenAI.
- **Don’t:** use the logo **without permission** or outside the terms; misrepresent the relationship, **imply endorsement**, or confuse users about sponsorship; use the logo **more prominently than your own**; place it on merchandise; **modify** it; incorporate it into your own branding or design a similar logo.
- Wordmark / Blossom: don’t stretch, crop, recolor, add effects, use unapproved variations, or use the Blossom as primary branding.
- **Brand partnerships / co-branded materials** “must undergo an approval process by both brands.”
- Usage terms: “Marks” includes names, logos, icons, and design elements. Permission is non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable at any time; OpenAI may review use and require changes; do not feature their Marks more prominently than your own; follow updates.
- Do not put model names in app titles; do not use the GPT brand in app, product, developer, or company names.
- Logo permission / questions beyond the listed cases: **partnercomms@openai.com**. Legal: **legal@openai.com**.

### 3. If no official logo license is published

Guidelines **are** published. They are **not** a clear grant to ship Codex/OpenAI artwork inside Agrypnos. Treat setup-card logos as **permission-required**.

## Cursor (Anysphere)

**Final URLs:**

- Brand kit: https://cursor.com/brand
- Marketplace Publisher Terms (publishers only): https://cursor.com/marketplace-publisher-terms
- Product Terms of Service: https://cursor.com/terms-of-service

### 1. Is the logo allowed for a third-party integrator?

**No published license** for Agrypnos (a separate Mac app, not a Cursor Marketplace plugin). The brand page lets people **download** logos, app icons, and avatars “to represent Cursor consistently and accurately.” It does not grant third-party products the right to display those assets in their own UI.

### 2. What the official pages require

https://cursor.com/brand:

- Logos in 2D (default) and 2.5D; horizontal lockup preferred; cube or wordmark separately.
- App icons and avatars in listed variants.
- **Name:** “Refer to us as Cursor. Not Cursor AI or Cursor Code.”

That is naming + asset availability. There is no “compatible with,” no “you may use these in third-party apps,” no “do not imply endorsement” paragraph on that page.

Marketplace Publisher Terms §4.6 apply **only if you publish a Plugin on the Cursor Marketplace**. Agrypnos is not that. Those terms say publisher use of Anysphere’s name, trademarks, or logos **must comply with the Brand Guidelines**; call the product “Cursor” (not “Cursor AI” / “Cursor Code”); Anysphere **may revoke** trademark use at any time; Marketplace removal revokes it automatically.

Product Terms of Service §5.1: Anysphere retains all IP in the Service; **“There are no implied licenses in these Terms.”**

### 3. If no official logo license is published

**None is published** for a third-party menu-bar extra. Do not treat cursor.com/brand downloads as a license to ship the cube/icon in Agrypnos.

## Telegram (Notif, if logos are added)

**Final URLs:**

- Logos / press: https://telegram.org/tour/screenshots
- Press hub (points at the same logos/screenshots page): https://telegram.org/press
- API Terms (third-party **Telegram clients**): https://core.telegram.org/api/terms

### 1. Is the logo allowed for a third-party integrator?

**Conditional, and not as Agrypnos’s own logo.**

https://telegram.org/tour/screenshots:

> Please feel free to use these Telegram logos for article illustrations, graphs, “forward to Telegram” buttons, etc. Just make sure people understand you’re not representing Telegram officially.

A Notif card that means “alerts go to **your** Telegram bot” is closer to that “forward to Telegram” identification than to using Clyde-style branding as Agrypnos’s identity — **if** the card stays Agrypnos’s, uses Telegram’s official files, and does not look like an official Telegram app.

https://core.telegram.org/api/terms §2.4 is aimed at **third-party Telegram client apps**:

> You must not use the official Telegram logo for your app. Both the Telegram brand and its logo are registered trademarks protected by law in almost every country.

§2.3: do not put “Telegram” in the **app title** unless preceded by “Unofficial.” Agrypnos is not named Telegram and is not a Telegram client. That clause still forbids using Telegram’s logo **as the Agrypnos app icon / Agrypnos identity**.

### 2. What the official pages require

- Allowed examples: article illustrations, graphs, “forward to Telegram” buttons.
- Must not represent Telegram officially.
- Screenshots on that page are CC0; the **logos** are not described as CC0.
- Do not use the official logo for **your** app (API Terms).

### 3. If no official logo license is published

A limited press-style permission **is** published on the logos page. It is not a blank check for product chrome. Keep it identification-only, unofficial, not Agrypnos’s own mark.

## Discord (Notif, if logos are added)

**Final URLs:**

- Brand Guidelines: https://discord.com/branding
- Developer Terms (bots / APIs): https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/8562894815383-Discord-Developer-Terms-of-Service
- (Social SDK marketing is a different product; still points at discord.com/branding: https://docs.discord.com/developers/discord-social-sdk/how-to/market-your-integration)

### 1. Is the logo allowed for a third-party integrator?

**Conditional.** Discord’s brand page says **feel free to use the official logo** in color, black, or white, and then lists legal dos and don’ts. Developer Terms grant a **limited, revocable** license to display Discord Brand Features for **required attribution** or **incidental promotion** of an Application that uses Discord’s APIs, following the Brand Guidelines. That is closer to a published integrator license than OpenCode or Cursor. It still forbids looking like Discord built, sponsors, or is Agrypnos.

### 2. What the official pages require

https://discord.com/branding:

- Feel free to use the logo in color, black, or white.
- Clyde **symbol** only when the Discord brand is already clearly visible / well established elsewhere.
- **Do not** edit, change, distort, recolor, or reconfigure the logo.
- Marks and Brand Assets are Discord Inc.’s. You must have permission **except as permitted on that page**.

Legal list on the same page (live text, 2026-10-06):

- Always follow the Brand Guidelines.
- You may use the Marks to inform people that you are a Discord user and/or have a Discord server; use **must not imply that you are Discord, or somehow sponsored by or acting on behalf of Discord**.
- “Discord” is an **adjective** for your server or username on Discord.
- Digital-only (not merchandise).
- **Do not** incorporate Discord Marks (or confusingly similar marks) into a server name, **your brand, company name, URL, event, product name (including any bots developed for the Discord platform), logo**, social account, or trademark.
- Do not adopt confusingly similar trade dress; do not imitate Discord’s look and feel; do not use Marks inconsistently with Discord’s Terms / Community Guidelines; **no merchandise**.

Developer Terms §8:

- Limited, revocable, non-transferable license **while the Terms are in effect** to display Discord Brand Features for required attribution or incidental promotion, **only** per the Terms and Brand Guidelines. Discord has **sole discretion** whether the use complies.
- **No statement** suggesting partnership, sponsorship, or endorsement without **prior written approval** in each instance.
- On termination: **cease all use** of Discord Brand Features.

### 3. If no official logo license is published

A license **is** published (brand page + Developer Terms). Setup-card use would still have to stay unmodified official artwork, digital-only, Agrypnos-branded, and clearly **not** Discord-endorsed. Do not put Discord’s mark into Agrypnos’s own name or app icon.

## What this note does not do

- It does not approve shipping any logo.
- It does not claim nominative fair use, implied license, or “everyone else does it.”
- It does not review files already in `docs/readme/logos/` (out of this question).
- It is not a lawyer’s opinion. Permission emails, if anyone asks: Anthropic **marketing@anthropic.com**; OpenAI logos **partnercomms@openai.com**.
